How prices of two assets move together over time. +1 means they move in lockstep, −1 means they move opposite, 0 means independent. Calculated on a 63-day rolling window of daily returns.

// AVG COUPLING

An asset's mean absolute correlation to the other 7 assets. High coupling means this asset is moving with the rest of the market. Low coupling means it offers diversification.

// REGIME

STABLE: assets move mostly independently.
STRESS: correlations are rising, market stress is building.
CRISIS: assets are moving together, diversification breaks down.
